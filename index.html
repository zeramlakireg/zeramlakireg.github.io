Personal backup and recovery

Mariner Assistant Backup is a personal Windows utility that saves selected assistant settings, instructions, skills, support code and documentation to the owner's Google account.

It is maintained for one owner's personal use. There are no public sign-ups or commercial services.

How it works

The utility prepares a local backup, encrypts its contents and uploads it to the application's hidden data area in Google Drive. It downloads and checks the uploaded copy to verify that the backup can be decrypted and matches the local archive.

After the owner connects the Google account, a Windows scheduled task runs the backup when the computer is on and the owner is logged in.

Google access

The application requests permission to read and write its own hidden Google Drive app data. This allows it to create, list, upload and retrieve backup files. The permission does not grant access to ordinary Google Drive documents or folders.

The backup excludes credentials, email, vessel folders, indexes and caches. The backup job does not call an AI API.

Privacy and support

The privacy policy explains the data handled, encryption, retention and removal of Google access.

For support or privacy enquiries, use the user support email shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
